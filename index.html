Art & Music, United02 / 05
The Vision

Beyond The Gallery,
Into The Marketplace

For 15 years my paintings have lived on the walls of Guitar Center Hollywood. Now I see an incredible opportunity to bring my artwork beyond the gallery and into the marketplace, through apparel, hoodies, straps, accessories, and immersive visual experiences.

My vision is to unite my two greatest passions, art and music, by transforming everyday products into distinctive, emotionally connected works of art that inspire musicians, celebrate individuality, and create new revenue for Guitar Center.
